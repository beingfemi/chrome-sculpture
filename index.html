Chrome Sculpture is a study in reflection. Four forms, and the room they reflect, written in code and generated at runtime.

Chrome Sculpture

Four forms, one material. Every surface here is generated at runtime — no meshes, no textures, no photographs. The metal has nothing of its own to show, so what you see is a studio painted into a 2048×1024 canvas and wrapped around the world. All four sculptures are evaluated over one 448×80 grid and share a single mesh, so they do not cut between forms — they flow.
